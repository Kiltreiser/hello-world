TagFixer

Fix your music tags in three steps

  1. Pick your music folder and press Scan & look up. Every album is looked up on MusicBrainz (about one album per second - large libraries take a while, and you can start reviewing straight away).
  2. Check the list. Confident matches are ticked for you. Click any album to see exactly what will change, pick a different edition, or paste a MusicBrainz link.
  3. Press Apply. Only ticked albums are written. Every batch can be undone.

Soundtracks and various-artist compilations are tagged so Plex/Plexamp file them correctly (album artist, compilation flag and release type). See Settings for how soundtracks are grouped.
